# BizCard Studio

> Your digital business card — share with a scan.

## What's bundled

- **vCard 4.0 generator** — full spec including photo embedding, birthday, and 7 social profile types (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, GitHub, Telegram, WhatsApp, Facebook).
- **Live preview** — see how the contact card looks before saving the QR.
- **Print-ready PDF** — drop onto a 90×54mm card template with bleed for traditional paper backup.
- **Single-user and multi-user (agency) modes** — agencies can manage cards for many employees.
- **Templates** — business-card themed (classic dark gray, executive burgundy + cream).

## Who buys this?

Sales professionals, consultants, freelancers, agencies offering “digital identity setup” to their clients.

## Selling angles

- Stop printing 500 cards every quarter and throwing 400 away.
- Update your job title once — every existing QR keeps working.
- Embed your face into the contact — recipients see who they just met.
- Looks premium next to a competitor's printed card.
