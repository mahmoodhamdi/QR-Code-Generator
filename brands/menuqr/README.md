# MenuQR Pro

> Digital QR menus for restaurants and cafés. Built on the QR Generator codebase, tailored for hospitality.

## What's bundled

- **Per-table QR generator** — create QR codes for table #1 through #50 in one click, each pointing to your menu URL with a `?table=N` parameter so you know which seat scanned.
- **Multi-language menus** — generate separate QRs for English and Arabic versions of the same menu URL.
- **WiFi sharing built-in** — guests scan one QR to join WiFi, another to see the menu.
- **Print-ready PDF export** — drag onto a table-tent template with bleed margins, crop marks, and CMYK conversion notes.
- **Restaurant-themed templates** — warm amber, café gray, fine-dining burgundy preloaded.
