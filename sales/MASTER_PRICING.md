## White-label rights

If the customer wants to resell their bundle to **their own clients** (instead of using it themselves), add **+50%** to the base price. This grants:

- Perpetual right to redistribute compiled binaries / hosted versions to up to 25 unique clients.
- For unlimited clients: +100% to base price (negotiable above 25).
